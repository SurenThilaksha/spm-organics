Our curry leaves powder captures the warm, citrusy aroma of fresh **karapincha** — the leaf
that starts almost every Sri Lankan curry.

Young leaves are hand-picked, washed, slowly dried in the shade to keep their deep green
colour, then stone-ground in small batches into a fine powder. There is no salt, colour,
preservative or filler — just the leaf.

Use it as a convenient everyday alternative to fresh leaves, or as a simple way to add more
greens to your diet. A little goes a long way.
